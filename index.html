What IRIS Does

IRIS is an AI agent that connects to your Google Workspace — Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Docs, Google Slides, and Google Drive — to help you complete everyday tasks such as searching and summarizing emails, managing calendar events, and creating or editing documents and presentations. IRIS only takes action based on your explicit requests.

Privacy Policy

With your explicit authorization via Google OAuth, IRIS may read and/or write data in the Google services you connect, strictly limited to the scopes you approve during sign-in.

Data accessed through your Google account is used only to perform the specific actions you request within the application. We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties.

Access is authorized via OAuth tokens, which are stored securely and used only to facilitate your requested actions. You may revoke IRIS's access at any time via your Google Account permissions page.
